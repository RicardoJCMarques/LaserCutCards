Laser Cut Cards

Describe the sheet you have and how to divide it. You get SVG files sized in real millimetres, so nothing needs re-scaling after import.

  1. Stock. Enter the sheet size and pick columns and rows. The card is the cell, so the division is also the shape: 2 x 3 gives portrait cards, 3 x 2 landscape ones.
  2. Design. Choose faces or the back, and how many cards. What you see here is exactly what Export writes.
  3. Output. Each side has its own operations. For two-sided cards, use Stock squaring file to trim your stock, engrave faces, flip the sheet left to right against the same origin, run backs, and cut.

The whole design lives in the address bar - copy it to share or reproduce it exactly.
